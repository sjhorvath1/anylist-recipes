Protein Peanut Butter Cheesecake

A high-protein, no-bake cheesecake made with blended cottage cheese and sugar-free cheesecake pudding mix. Quick to assemble, creamy, and satisfying — serve chilled or eat right away. Makes 2 servings.

Yield: 2 servings

Ingredients

Cheesecake Base

  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 1 (1 oz) box sugar-free cheesecake instant pudding mix
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter powder
  • 2–3 tbsp sugar-free syrup

Peanut Butter Topping

  • 2 tbsp peanut butter powder
  • 1–2 tbsp sugar-free syrup

Optional

  • Crushed Oreo cookies, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Add cottage cheese to a blender and blend until completely smooth.
  2. Add the sugar-free cheesecake pudding mix, 2 tbsp peanut butter powder, and 2–3 tbsp sugar-free syrup to the blender. Blend until fully combined.
  3. Divide the mixture evenly into 2 serving cups or small containers.
  4. In a small bowl, mix the remaining 2 tbsp peanut butter powder with 1–2 tbsp sugar-free syrup until it forms a drizzleable topping. Drizzle over each cup.
  5. Top with crushed Oreo if using. Chill until ready to eat, or enjoy immediately.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (1 of 2): 171 calories | Protein: 20g | Fat: 1.2g | Carbohydrates: 17g | Fiber: 1.9g

Source: USDA FoodData Central. Excludes optional Oreo garnish. Sugar-free syrup treated as negligible calories.
