Cottage Cheese Elote Dip

Makes approximately 6 servings

A high-protein elote-style dip with blended cottage cheese as its creamy base, loaded with shredded chicken, roasted corn, jalapeño, cilantro, queso fresco, and smoky chipotle heat. Perfect with chips, tacos, or veggies. Adapted from @rileysstupidrecipes.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) street corn or whole kernel corn, drained
  • 1 cup shredded chicken breast, cooked
  • 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 jalapeño, diced
  • 2 tbsp Mexican crema
  • 1 tub (16 oz) 2% cottage cheese
  • 3 tbsp Chipotle Tabasco sauce
  • 2 tbsp Tajín seasoning
  • 1/3 cup queso fresco, crumbled
  • Juice of 1 lime

Instructions

  1. Add the cottage cheese to a blender and blend until completely smooth.
  2. Pour the blended cottage cheese into a large mixing bowl. Add the shredded chicken, cilantro, jalapeño, and drained corn.
  3. Mix in the crema, Chipotle Tabasco, Tajín, crumbled queso fresco, and lime juice.
  4. Stir everything together thoroughly until fully combined.
  5. Refrigerate for 1 to 2 hours to allow the flavors to meld.
  6. Before serving, top with extra corn, a sprinkle of Tajín, and additional crumbled queso fresco if desired.
