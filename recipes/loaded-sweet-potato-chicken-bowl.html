Loaded Sweet Potato with Chicken Bowl

A microwave-baked sweet potato topped with nonfat Greek yogurt, air-fried tequila lime and flaming hot seasoned chicken thighs, avocado, charred sweet corn, pickled red onion, cotija cheese, jalapeño, pico de gallo, cilantro, and lime.

Ingredients

Sweet Potato Base

  • 1 large sweet potato
  • 1/4 cup nonfat Greek yogurt

Chicken

  • 10 oz boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • Cusos tequila lime seasoning, to taste
  • Cusos flaming hot seasoning, to taste

Toppings

  • 1/2 avocado
  • 4 tbsp charred sweet corn
  • 1 tbsp pickled red onion
  • 2 tbsp cotija cheese
  • Jalapeño, sliced, to taste
  • 1/2 cup pico de gallo
  • Cilantro, for garnish
  • Lime wedges, for serving

Instructions

  1. Poke holes in the sweet potato.
  2. Wrap the sweet potato in a wet paper towel.
  3. Microwave the sweet potato for 8 minutes, or cook using your preferred method.
  4. Coat the chicken thighs in avocado oil and the tequila lime and flaming hot seasonings.
  5. Air fry the chicken thighs at 400 degrees F for 15 to 20 minutes.
  6. Slice open the sweet potato, top with Greek yogurt, layer with chicken and remaining toppings as desired, and enjoy.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (1 serving, USDA FoodData Central): 970 calories, 70 g protein, 54 g fat, 58 g carbohydrates, 14 g fiber.
