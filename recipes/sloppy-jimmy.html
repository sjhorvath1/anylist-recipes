Sloppy Jimmy

A Scottish-American mashup: lamb mince and onion in a rich, tangy sauce piled into buttered well-fired morning rolls and topped with a melted smoky cheese slice. A "pimp up" of the classic Scottish roll and mince, crossed with an American Sloppy Joe.

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • Olive oil, for frying
  • 500 g lamb mince
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 green capsicum (bell pepper), finely diced
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon Scottish seven spice powder
  • 1 tablespoon tomato puree
  • 1 tablespoon Vegemite or Marmite
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tablespoon Irn-Bru hot sauce
  • 200 ml beef or lamb stock
  • Splash of malt vinegar or chili malt vinegar
  • Salt, to taste
  • Well-fired morning rolls (4), slathered in butter, to serve
  • Smoky cheese slices (4), to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat a splash of olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the diced onion and green pepper with a pinch of salt and saute until softened. Remove from the pan and set aside.
  2. In the same pan, saute the lamb mince over medium-high heat until caramelized and golden.
  3. Add the softened onion and pepper back into the pan with the lamb. Stir in the brown sugar and seven spice powder and saute until fragrant.
  4. Deglaze the pan with 100 ml of the stock, scraping up any browned bits.
  5. Add the tomato puree, Vegemite or Marmite, Worcestershire sauce, and Irn-Bru hot sauce. Pour in the remaining 100 ml of stock and stir to combine.
  6. Simmer until the sauce has thickened to a sloppy consistency. Season to taste and finish with a splash of malt vinegar or chili malt vinegar.
  7. Pile the filling into buttered well-fired morning rolls. Top each with a smoky cheese slice and melt using a blow torch or under the grill for a couple of minutes. Serve immediately.
