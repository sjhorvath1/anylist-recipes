Rigatoni alla Vodka

Rigatoni tossed in a creamy vodka tomato sauce with Calabrian chili, cottage cheese, and Parmigiano Reggiano, topped with air-fried marinated chicken breast.

Yield: 6 servings

Ingredients

Chicken/Marinade

  • 24 oz thin-sliced chicken breast
  • 1 Tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 2 tsp coarse salt
  • 1 Tbsp Calabrian chili, diced
  • 1 Tbsp tomato paste

Pasta

  • 400 g rigatoni
  • 1 cup reserved pasta water

Vodka Sauce

  • 15 g olive oil
  • 1 sweet onion, diced
  • 5-7 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 2 Tbsp Calabrian chili, diced
  • 4 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 28 oz canned San Marzano tomatoes
  • 1 Tbsp fish sauce (optional)
  • 100 g low-fat milk
  • 2 shots vodka
  • 300 g blended cottage cheese
  • 50 g Parmigiano Reggiano, grated

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken breast with the minced garlic, onion powder, salt, diced Calabrian chili, tomato paste, and a splash of water.
  2. Heat 15 g olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced onion and sliced garlic and 2 Tbsp diced Calabrian chili. Simmer 15 minutes until soft. Stir in 4 Tbsp tomato paste and cook 3 minutes. Add the canned San Marzano tomatoes and fish sauce (optional). Break up the tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes until thick. Blend with the low-fat milk until smooth.
  3. Preheat air fryer to 390°F (200°C). Cook the chicken 9-12 minutes, or until done.
  4. Boil salted water and cook the rigatoni until slightly underdone. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta water.
  5. Pour the sauce into a pan over medium heat, add the vodka, and simmer 2 minutes. Add the pasta and 3/4 cup of the reserved pasta water, and simmer until reduced. The vodka can be left out and the dish will still be delicious, but it will not technically be a vodka sauce.
  6. Remove from heat and stir in the blended cottage cheese, grated Parmigiano Reggiano, and 1/4 cup of the reserved pasta water. Toss until smooth.
  7. Top with the chicken and enjoy.

Nutrition

Per serving (makes 6): 515 calories | 43 g protein | 60 g carbohydrates | 10 g fat
