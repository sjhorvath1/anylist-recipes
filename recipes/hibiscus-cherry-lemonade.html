Hibiscus Cherry Lemonade

A creamy, tart blended lemonade with hibiscus tea, frozen cherries, and a glug of olive oil for body. Makes about 32 oz (roughly 2 large servings).

Yield: 2 servings (about 32 oz total)

Ingredients

  • 12-16 oz hibiscus tea, chilled
  • 1 whole lemon, cut into chunks (peel on)
  • 1 cup frozen cherries
  • 3 tsp honey, plus more to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil (a glug)
  • Pinch of flaky sea salt
  • Ice, for serving
  • Lemon wedges, for serving

Instructions

  1. Start by making the hibiscus tea. The easiest method is cold brewing it overnight in the fridge, but you can also steep it with hot water and pop it into the freezer for 30 to 40 minutes until chilled.
  2. To a high-speed blender, add the lemon chunks, chilled hibiscus tea, frozen cherries, honey, olive oil, and flaky sea salt.
  3. Blend for about 1 minute, just enough for everything to become fully incorporated and creamy. Do not overblend, otherwise the lemon pith can start to turn bitter.
  4. Strain into a large ice-filled glass, add a few lemon wedges, and enjoy immediately. Store leftovers in a mason jar in the fridge; it will separate, but shake it and pour over ice. Drink within 5 days.

Notes

  • You can always add more honey to make it less tart.
  • The olive oil makes this creamy and smooth - don't skip it.
  • Would also be incredible topped with sparkling water for a spritz.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (2 servings, ~16 oz each): 150 calories | 1 g protein | 7 g fat | 25 g carbohydrates | 4 g fiber
