Crockpot Mongolian Beef and Noodles

Source: @406_bbq

Tender chuck roast in a sweet and savory Mongolian-style sauce, tossed with lo mein noodles. The beef is seared in a skillet first for extra color, then finished in the slow cooker (crockpot).

Ingredients

Beef

  • 2 pounds chuck roast, thinly sliced
  • Salt, to taste (for seasoning the beef)
  • Black pepper, to taste (for seasoning the beef)
  • Garlic powder, to taste (for seasoning the beef)
  • 4 tablespoons butter, for searing

Sauce

  • 1 cup low-sodium beef broth
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium shoyu (soy sauce)
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper

Slurry

  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons cold water

Noodles and Garnish

  • 12 ounces precooked lo mein noodles
  • Green onions, sliced into 1-inch pieces
  • Sesame seeds, optional
  • Chili crisp oil, optional

Instructions

  1. Season the beef with salt, black pepper, and garlic powder, or your favorite seasoning. Heat a skillet over high heat with the butter and sear the beef in small batches for 1 to 2 minutes per side. You are building color, not cooking it through.
  2. Transfer the beef to the crockpot. Deglaze the skillet with a splash of the measured beef broth, scrape up the browned bits, and pour everything into the crockpot.
  3. Add the remaining beef broth, shoyu, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and black pepper. Stir gently.
  4. Cover and cook on LOW for 4 to 5 hours or on HIGH for 2.5 to 3.5 hours. Start checking early so the thin beef stays tender without falling apart.
  5. Whisk the cornstarch with the cold water to make a slurry. Stir the slurry into the sauce and cook on HIGH for 15 to 20 minutes, until thick and glossy.
  6. Slice the green onions into 1-inch pieces. Separate the precooked noodles and add them directly to the crockpot. Toss everything together and heat on HIGH for 5 to 10 minutes.
  7. Finish with sliced green onions, sesame seeds, and chili crisp oil if you want some heat. Serve immediately; do not leave the noodles on KEEP WARM, as they will keep absorbing the sauce.
