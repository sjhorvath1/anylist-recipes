Chicken Shawarma Rice Bowls

Yogurt-marinated shawarma chicken thighs packed into loaf pans and oven-baked, sliced thin and served over rice with oven-roasted peppers, zucchini, and onion and a creamy garlic yogurt sauce. Makes 8 meal-prep bowls; leftovers freeze well.

Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

Chicken Shawarma

  • 1800 g boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 320 g 5% Greek yogurt
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 30 g tomato paste

Shawarma Spice Blend

  • 2 Tbsp Morton kosher salt
  • 1 Tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 Tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 Tbsp ground coriander
  • 1 Tbsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground allspice

Roasted Veggies

  • 5 red bell peppers, diced
  • 2 large zucchini, sliced into half-moons
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1 tsp Morton kosher salt

Creamy Garlic Yogurt Sauce

  • 320 g 5% Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 3-4 cloves garlic, grated
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Rice

  • 580 g long grain white rice, dry

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix 320 g 5% Greek yogurt, 2 Tbsp olive oil, 30 g tomato paste, and the shawarma spice blend: 2 Tbsp Morton kosher salt, 1 Tbsp smoked paprika, 1 Tbsp cumin, 1 Tbsp coriander, 1 Tbsp garlic powder, 2 tsp black pepper, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cinnamon, and 1 tsp allspice.
  2. Add 1800 g boneless skinless chicken thighs and mix until coated. Pack tightly into 2 loaf pans. Cover and marinate at least 1 hour, ideally 12+ hours. The longer it marinates, the better.
  3. Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C). Uncover loaf pans and bake for 50-60 minutes, or until the center of the chicken reaches at least 175°F (79°C).
  4. While the chicken bakes, cook 580 g long grain white rice in a rice cooker.
  5. Whisk together 320 g 5% Greek yogurt, 1 tsp olive oil, 1 lemon (zested and juiced), and 3-4 cloves grated garlic to make the creamy garlic yogurt sauce. Add salt and black pepper to taste.
  6. When the chicken is done, pour the rendered juices from both pans into a bowl and set aside. Let the chicken rest in the pans for 15-30 minutes.
  7. Toss 5 diced red bell peppers, 2 large zucchini sliced into half-moons, and 1 large diced onion with 1 tsp Morton kosher salt and a few spoonfuls of the reserved chicken juices.
  8. Spread the vegetables on a sheet pan and roast at 425°F (218°C) for 20-30 minutes, until tender.
  9. Flip the chicken onto a cutting board and slice thin.
  10. Mix the remaining chicken juices into the rice, or spoon them over each bowl before adding sauce.
  11. Divide into 8 bowls with rice, roasted veggies, and garlic yogurt sauce. Enjoy, and freeze leftovers.
