Honey Garlic Soy Chicken

Crispy seared skin-on chicken thighs finished in a glossy honey, garlic, and low-sodium soy sauce glaze with scallions.

Yield: 8 servings

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 4 lb skin-on chicken thighs, bones removed
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 2-3 Tbsp neutral oil

Sauce

  • 12 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 bunch scallions, sliced

Instructions

  1. Pat the chicken dry, then season it with the salt and pepper and coat it with the cornstarch.
  2. Sear the chicken skin-side down for 4-5 minutes, until crispy.
  3. Flip the chicken and cook it to an internal temperature of 165°F, then remove it from the pan.
  4. Add the garlic, honey, and soy sauce to the pan and let it bubble until glossy.
  5. Return the chicken to the pan, coat it in the sauce, and finish with the scallions.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (8 servings, from USDA FoodData Central): 655 calories | 40 g protein | 42 g fat | 29 g carbohydrates | 0.7 g fiber

The serving count is an estimate, not stated in the original recipe. The estimate uses raw skin-on thigh values, 2.5 Tbsp oil (the midpoint of 2-3 Tbsp), and an assumed 100 g of scallions, and excludes salt. It does not adjust for fat left in the pan.
