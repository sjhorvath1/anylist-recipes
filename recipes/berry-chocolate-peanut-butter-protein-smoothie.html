Berry Chocolate Peanut Butter Protein Smoothie

A single-serving high-protein smoothie made with frozen mixed berries, collagen, powdered peanut butter, and a Fairlife Nutrition Plan chocolate shake.

Yield: 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen mixed berries
  • 4 Tbsp Vital Proteins collagen protein powder
  • 2 Tbsp PBfit peanut butter powder
  • 1 bottle Fairlife Nutrition Plan chocolate shake

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving: 435 calories | 57 g protein | 44 g carbohydrates | 6 g fat | 14 g fiber | 26 g sugar (berries, collagen, and PBfit estimated from USDA FoodData Central; Fairlife Nutrition Plan values from the product label)
