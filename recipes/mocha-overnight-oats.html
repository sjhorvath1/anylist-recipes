Mocha Overnight Oats with Protein Yogurt Topping

```

Chocolate-espresso overnight oats topped with a creamy vanilla protein yogurt layer. Gluten-free, refined sugar-free, and dairy-free. Make the night before and refrigerate overnight for a high-protein breakfast ready to go in the morning.

Yield: 4 servings

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Total Time: 8 hours 10 minutes (including overnight chill)

Ingredients

Oat Base

  • 1 cup rolled oats (certified organic, glyphosate-free)
  • 3 tablespoons raw cacao powder, plus extra for garnish
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons chia seeds
  • 3 tablespoons espresso, cooled
  • 1 1/4 cups full-fat almond milk
  • 2 tablespoons raw honey
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 pinch pink Himalayan salt

Protein Yogurt Topping

  • 1/2 tablespoon raw honey
  • 1/4 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 30 g vanilla protein powder (clean, organic, gum-free)
  • 1 1/2 cups full-fat dairy-free yogurt

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the rolled oats, cacao powder, chia seeds, and pink Himalayan salt. Mix until well combined.
  2. Add the cooled espresso, almond milk, 2 tablespoons of raw honey, and 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract. Stir well to combine.
  3. Transfer the oat mixture to a sealable glass container and spread evenly.
  4. To make the protein yogurt topping, whisk together the dairy-free yogurt, protein powder, 1/2 tablespoon of raw honey, and 1/4 teaspoon of vanilla extract until smooth.
  5. Spread the protein yogurt mixture evenly over the top of the oat base.
  6. Sift a little additional cacao powder over the top as garnish. Seal the container and refrigerate for at least 8 hours or overnight before serving.
```
