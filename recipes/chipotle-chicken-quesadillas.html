Chipotle Chicken Quesadillas

Source: @fairfiteats

```

High-protein meal prep quesadillas made with slow-cooker chipotle chicken, fat-free mozzarella, and a creamy cottage cheese chipotle sauce. Makes 10 quesadillas. Freezer-friendly for up to 3 months.

Yield: 10 quesadillas

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 40 oz (1135g) chicken breast
  • 1 tbsp (15g) garlic salt
  • 1 tbsp (7g) black pepper
  • 7 oz (200g) canned chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 2 tbsp (40g) honey

Quesadillas

  • 6 cups (670g) fat-free mozzarella, shredded
  • 10g fresh chives, chopped
  • 10 low-calorie tortillas

Chipotle Sauce

  • 2 cups (450g) 0% fat cottage cheese
  • 3 tbsp (45g) light mayo
  • 3 tbsp (50g) minced chipotle peppers
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 0.5 tbsp (7g) garlic salt
  • 0.5 tbsp (3g) black pepper
  • 2 tbsp (40g) honey
  • 0.5 cup (120g) fat-free milk

Instructions

  1. Place chicken breasts in a slow cooker. Season with garlic salt and black pepper.
  2. Add the canned chipotle peppers, lime juice, and honey over the chicken. Cook on low for 6-8 hours or high for 3-4 hours until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
  3. While wearing gloves, shred the chicken directly in the slow cooker, mixing it with all the juices and chipotle peppers.
  4. Make the chipotle sauce: blend together the cottage cheese, light mayo, minced chipotle peppers, lime juice, garlic salt, black pepper, honey, and fat-free milk until smooth.
  5. Heat a pan over medium heat. Lay a tortilla flat and add a layer of shredded mozzarella, then a portion of chipotle chicken, a drizzle of chipotle sauce, and a sprinkle of fresh chives.
  6. Fold the tortilla in half and cook for 2-3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese is melted. Repeat for all 10 quesadillas.

Freezing Instructions

  1. Wrap each quesadilla in aluminum foil or place in an airtight freezer bag.
  2. Freeze for up to 3 months.

Reheating Instructions

  1. Remove a frozen quesadilla from the freezer and wrap it in a wet paper towel.
  2. Microwave on a plate for 3-4 minutes.
  3. For a crispy finish, toast in a pan or air fryer for a few minutes after microwaving.

Nutrition

Per quesadilla (as provided by creator): 400 calories | Protein: 60g | Carbohydrates: 26g | Fat: 6g
```
