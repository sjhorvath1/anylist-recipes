Chocolate Peanut Butter Crunch Bar

```

A no-bake cereal bar with a crispy peanut butter base and a dark chocolate topping. Stored in the fridge or freezer, these bars come together quickly with just a handful of pantry staples. Makes one 9x13 pan, about 16 bars.

Yield: 16 bars

Ingredients

Base Layer

  • 4 cups heritage flakes (or other natural corn flake cereal)
  • 3/4 cup natural peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup maple syrup
  • 3 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Topping

  • 1 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil

Instructions

  1. Line a 9x13 baking dish with parchment paper.
  2. Pour the corn flakes into a large bowl and set aside.
  3. Combine the peanut butter, maple syrup, and coconut oil in a small saucepan over low heat. Stir until smooth and fully combined, then remove from heat. Stir in the vanilla extract and salt.
  4. Pour the peanut butter mixture over the corn flakes and stir until all the flakes are evenly coated.
  5. Transfer the mixture to the prepared baking dish and press down firmly into an even layer. Place in the freezer until firm.
  6. While the base sets, melt the dark chocolate chips and coconut oil together until smooth.
  7. Remove the base from the freezer and drizzle or spread the melted chocolate evenly over the top. Return to the freezer until the chocolate is firm.
  8. Remove from the freezer, cut into bars, and store in an airtight container in the fridge or freezer.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (1 of 16 bars): 224 calories | Fat: 13.7g | Protein: 4g | Carbohydrates: 22.5g | Fiber: 1.7g

```
