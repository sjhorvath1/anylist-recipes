Lemongrass Chicken Meatball Bowl

Air-fried lemongrass chicken meatballs served over jasmine rice with a fried egg, fresh vegetables, and a tangy fish sauce dressing. High-protein, Southeast Asian-inspired bowl. Makes 4 servings.

Yields: 4 servings

Cook time: 22 minutes

Total time: About 40 minutes

Ingredients

Meatballs

  • 500g chicken breast mince
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 heaped tbsp lemongrass paste (or finely chopped fresh lemongrass, white part only)
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tbsp coconut sugar
  • 1/2 cup spring onions, finely chopped
  • 1/3 cup fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper

Dressing

  • 1/4 cup hot water
  • 1 tsp coriander stems, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 tsp rice wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp coconut sugar
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1/2 long red chili, deseeded and finely chopped

To Serve

  • 4 cups steamed jasmine rice
  • 4 eggs, fried
  • 1 cucumber, cut into matchsticks
  • 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup fresh coriander leaves

Instructions

  1. Make the meatballs: Combine all meatball ingredients in a large bowl and mix until just combined. Roll into large golf ball-sized portions or shape into patties.
  2. Air fry: Preheat the air fryer to 190 degrees C (375 degrees F). Lightly spray the meatballs with oil and arrange in a single layer without overcrowding. Cook for 10 to 12 minutes until golden brown, flipping at the 8-minute mark.
  3. Make the dressing: Combine the fish sauce, hot water, lime juice, rice wine vinegar, and coconut sugar and stir until the sugar is dissolved. Add the garlic, chili, and coriander stems. Stir and set aside.
  4. Prep the bowl components: Steam the jasmine rice. Slice the cucumber and carrot into matchsticks. Pick the mint and coriander leaves. Fry the eggs to your liking.
  5. Assemble: Add rice to each bowl, then top with meatballs, vegetables, herbs, and a fried egg. Drizzle generously with dressing and serve immediately.
