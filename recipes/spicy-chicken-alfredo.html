Spicy Chicken Alfredo

Slow cooker spicy shredded chicken tossed with pasta in a high-protein roasted red pepper alfredo sauce made from cottage cheese, cream cheese, and Parmigiano Reggiano. Chicken cooks in the slow cooker while the peppers and garlic roast in the oven; pasta finishes in the slow cooker. Freezer-friendly meal prep.

Yield: 10 servings

Ingredients

Spicy Slow Cooker Chicken

  • 900 g (32 oz) chicken breast, diced
  • 30 g (1/4 cup) Parmigiano Reggiano, shredded
  • 30 g (2 Tbsp) minced garlic
  • 1 Tbsp salt
  • 2 tsp crushed red pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 120 g (1/2 cup) chicken bone broth
  • 30 g (2 Tbsp) butter

Roasted Vegetables

  • 5 red bell peppers
  • 10 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp oil of choice

Red Pepper Alfredo Sauce

  • 800 g (3 1/3 cups) 2% cottage cheese
  • 120 g (1/2 cup) 1/3-fat cream cheese
  • 120 g (1 cup) Parmigiano Reggiano, shredded
  • 240 g (1 cup) 1% milk
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Red chili flakes, to taste

Pasta

  • 672 g (24 oz) pasta
  • 240 g (1 cup) reserved pasta water

Garnish

  • Fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Shred 30 g Parmigiano Reggiano and dice the chicken breast. Add both to the slow cooker with the minced garlic, salt, crushed red pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, cayenne pepper, chicken bone broth, and butter. Stir to coat the chicken.
  2. Cover and cook on high for 2-3 hours or low for 4-5 hours, until tender. Shred the chicken in the slow cooker once cooked through.
  3. While the chicken cooks, wrap the 10 garlic cloves in foil with 1 tsp oil. Roast the red bell peppers and the garlic at 450°F (230°C) for 30-50 minutes, until soft and slightly charred. Peel the peppers once cooled.
  4. Shred 120 g Parmigiano Reggiano and add to a blender with the roasted peppers, roasted garlic, cottage cheese, cream cheese, milk, and red chili flakes to taste. Season with salt and black pepper to taste and blend until smooth.
  5. Cook the pasta for half the package time. Reserve 240 g (1 cup) pasta water and drain.
  6. Add the half-cooked pasta, reserved pasta water, and blended sauce to the slow cooker. Stir to combine. Cover and cook on low for 15-20 minutes, until the pasta is tender and the sauce thickens.
  7. Divide into 10 servings, sprinkle with chopped parsley, and store leftovers in freezer-safe containers.
