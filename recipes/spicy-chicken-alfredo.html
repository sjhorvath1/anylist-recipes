Spicy Chicken Alfredo

Slow cooker spicy shredded chicken and pasta in a high-protein roasted red pepper Alfredo sauce made with cottage cheese, cream cheese, and Parmigiano Reggiano. Meal-prep friendly and freezer safe.

Yield: 10 servings

Ingredients

Spicy Slow Cooker Chicken

  • 900 g chicken breast, diced
  • 30 g Parmigiano Reggiano, shredded
  • 30 g garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp salt
  • 2 tsp crushed red pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 120 g chicken bone broth
  • 30 g butter

Pasta

  • 672 g pasta
  • 240 g pasta water, reserved

Roasted Vegetables

  • 5 red bell peppers
  • 10 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp oil of choice

Red Pepper Alfredo Sauce

  • 800 g 2% cottage cheese
  • 120 g 1/3-fat cream cheese
  • 120 g Parmigiano Reggiano, shredded
  • 240 g 1% milk
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Red chili flakes, to taste

Garnish

  • Fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Add the diced chicken breast and 30 g shredded Parmigiano Reggiano to the slow cooker with the minced garlic, salt, crushed red pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, cayenne pepper, chicken bone broth, and butter. Stir to coat the chicken. Cover and cook on high for 2 to 3 hours or on low for 4 to 5 hours, until tender. Shred the chicken in the slow cooker once cooked through.
  2. While the chicken cooks, wrap the 10 garlic cloves in foil with the oil. Roast the red bell peppers and the foil-wrapped garlic at 450°F (230°C) for 30 to 50 minutes, until soft and slightly charred. Peel the peppers once cooled.
  3. Add 120 g shredded Parmigiano Reggiano to a blender with the roasted peppers, roasted garlic, cottage cheese, cream cheese, milk, and red chili flakes to taste. Season with salt and black pepper to taste and blend until smooth.
  4. Cook the pasta for half the package time. Reserve 240 g pasta water, then drain.
  5. Add the half-cooked pasta, reserved pasta water, and blended sauce to the slow cooker. Stir to combine. Cover and cook on low for 15 to 20 minutes, until the pasta is tender and the sauce thickens.
  6. Divide into 10 servings, sprinkle with chopped parsley, and store leftovers in freezer-safe containers.
