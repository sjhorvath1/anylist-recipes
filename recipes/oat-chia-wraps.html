Oat and Chia Wraps

```

Healthy, flexible wraps made from just oats and chia seeds. Blend, rest, and pan-cook for a simple high-fiber base you can fill with anything.

Yield: 8 wraps

Prep time: 5 minutes (plus 15 minutes resting)

Cook time: 5 minutes per wrap

Ingredients

  • 10g chia seeds
  • 170g rolled oats
  • 500ml water
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Garlic powder, to taste
  • Neutral oil, for the pan

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients (chia seeds, oats, water, and seasonings) to a blender and blend until smooth.
  2. Let the mixture sit for 15 minutes to thicken.
  3. Add a little oil to a frying pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour about 1/3 cup of the batter into the pan and spread evenly.
  5. Cook until the edges lift off the pan, about 3 to 4 minutes. Flip and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes. Repeat with remaining batter.

Nutrition

Estimated per wrap (1 of 8): 87 calories | Protein: 3g | Fat: 2g | Carbohydrates: 15g | Fiber: 3g

Calculated from USDA FoodData Central. Does not include oil used for cooking or any fillings.
```
