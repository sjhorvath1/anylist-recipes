Day by Day Cafe Buckwheat Pancakes

Source: Day by Day Cafe

Day by Day Cafe's buckwheat pancake batter, scaled down to 1/8 of the restaurant prep batch for home cooking.

Yield: 6 servings (about 18 pancakes, 3 per serving)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 1/4 cups white flour
  • 1 cup buckwheat flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 oz butter, melted

Instructions

  1. Mix together the whole milk, eggs, and vanilla extract.
  2. Sift together the white flour, buckwheat flour, sugar, baking powder, and baking soda.
  3. Add the dry ingredients to the milk and egg mixture and mix completely.
  4. Add the 1 oz melted butter and mix it into the batter.

Nutrition
