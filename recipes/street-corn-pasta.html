Street Corn Pasta

A creamy ditalini pasta with charred street corn, seasoned chicken breast, cotija cheese, and Tajin, finished with Greek yogurt and fresh cilantro.

Yield: 10 servings

Ingredients

Street Corn

  • 900g (32oz) frozen corn
  • 180g (1 1/2 cups) cotija cheese
  • 120g (1/2 cup) 0% Greek yogurt (FAGE brand recommended)
  • 75g (1/3 cup) light sour cream
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • Handful of fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 limes, juiced and zested
  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 1 Tbsp Tajin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp onion powder

Chicken

  • 1150g (40oz) chicken breast, pounded thin
  • 15g (1 Tbsp) oil
  • Chili powder, to taste
  • Garlic powder, to taste
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Pasta

  • 900g (32oz) chicken bone broth
  • 680g (24oz) ditalini
  • 354g (12oz can) fat-free evaporated milk
  • Garlic powder, to taste
  • Salt, to taste

Finishing

  • 180g (3/4 cup) 0% Greek yogurt
  • 80g (2/3 cup) cotija cheese
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped, to taste
  • Tajin, to taste
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Spread 900g (32oz) frozen corn on a sheet pan. Broil on high for 4-5 minutes until charred. Transfer to a bowl, mix with 180g (1 1/2 cups) cotija cheese, 120g (1/2 cup) 0% Greek yogurt, 75g (1/3 cup) light sour cream, 6 cloves minced garlic, a handful of chopped cilantro, juice and zest from 2 limes, 1 Tbsp chili powder, 1 Tbsp Tajin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, and 1 tsp onion powder. Cover and refrigerate.
  2. Pound 1150g (40oz) chicken breast thin. Add to the same sheet pan, cover with 15g (1 Tbsp) oil, then season both sides with chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper. Bake at 375F (190C) for 30 minutes, then chop.
  3. Bring 900g (32oz) chicken bone broth to a boil in a large pot. Add 680g (24oz) ditalini and 354g (12oz can) fat-free evaporated milk, then season with garlic powder and salt to taste. Reduce to medium-low for 20 minutes or until the pasta is cooked and sauce clings to it. Stir frequently over the final 10 minutes to prevent sticking.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in the chopped chicken, 180g (3/4 cup) 0% Greek yogurt, chopped fresh cilantro, and Tajin to taste. Add in the street corn, 80g (2/3 cup) cotija cheese, and salt to taste. Rest a few minutes.
  5. Divide into 10 servings and enjoy.

Nutrition

Per serving (makes 10): 655 calories | 54g protein | 77g carbs | 15g fat
