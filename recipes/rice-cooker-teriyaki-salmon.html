Rice Cooker Teriyaki Salmon

A one-pot teriyaki salmon, rice, and broccoli meal made entirely in a rice cooker, finished with avocado and chili crisp.

Ingredients

Main

  • 1 cup rice (Japanese short-grain or medium grain recommended)
  • 1/2 lb salmon, uncooked
  • 3 tablespoons teriyaki sauce, plus more for drizzling (store-bought or homemade, see below)
  • 1 cup broccoli, fresh or frozen
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 avocado
  • Chili crisp, for drizzling, optional

Teriyaki Sauce (makes more than needed for this recipe)

  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons honey (or substitute brown sugar)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ginger, grated
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch, add more or less depending on thickness preference

Instructions

  1. Skip this step if using store-bought teriyaki sauce. In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine the soy sauce, water, honey (or brown sugar), minced garlic, grated ginger, and cornstarch. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened. Remove from heat.
  2. Rinse the rice thoroughly and place it in the rice cooker. Add the same amount of water as you normally would for the type of rice you are using.
  3. To the rice cooker, add the broccoli and salmon. Sprinkle the broccoli with the salt and pepper.
  4. Pour 3 tablespoons of teriyaki sauce directly on top of the salmon. Cover the rice cooker and allow to marinate in the fridge for at least 30 minutes, or overnight for the best flavor.
  5. Place the pot into the rice cooker. Close the rice cooker lid and cook on the regular white rice setting. Optionally, add the broccoli halfway through cooking for a more crisp texture.
  6. Serve, and drizzle with additional teriyaki sauce, half of an avocado, and some chili crisp.
