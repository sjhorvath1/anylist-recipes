Miso Chicken and Roasted Veggies

```

A sheet pan dinner featuring crispy-skinned bone-in chicken thighs glazed in a savory miso-honey sauce, roasted alongside baby potatoes, carrots, kalettes, bell peppers, and broccoli. The chicken drippings baste the vegetables as everything roasts together.

Yield: 6-8 servings
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 45-50 minutes
Total time: About 1 hour

Ingredients

Miso Sauce

  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup white miso paste
  • 2 tbsp honey (or maple syrup)
  • 1 tbsp unseasoned rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Black pepper, to taste

Chicken and Vegetables

  • 2.5 lbs bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 2 lbs baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 lb carrots, sliced into thick coins
  • 200g kalettes
  • 1-2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 small broccoli crown, cut into florets
  • Olive oil, for drizzling
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Optional Garnish

  • Sesame seeds, for garnish
  • Green onions, chopped, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Make the miso sauce: In a large bowl, whisk together olive oil, white miso paste, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and black pepper until smooth.
  3. Marinate the chicken: Add the bone-in chicken thighs to the miso sauce and toss until fully coated. Set aside.
  4. Place the halved baby potatoes and carrot coins on the prepared baking sheet. Drizzle or spray with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Roast for 20 minutes.
  5. Remove the pan from the oven and toss the potatoes and carrots. Add the bell peppers, broccoli florets, and kalettes to the pan. Place the miso-coated chicken thighs skin-side up on top of the vegetables. Spoon any remaining miso sauce over everything.
  6. Return to the oven and roast for 25-30 more minutes, until the chicken skin is golden and crisp and an internal thermometer reads 165 degrees F (74 degrees C).
  7. Optional: Switch the oven to broil for 2 minutes at the end for extra-crispy skin. Watch closely to avoid burning.
  8. Garnish with sesame seeds and chopped green onions before serving.
```
