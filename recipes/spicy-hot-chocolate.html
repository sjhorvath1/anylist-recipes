Spicy Hot Chocolate

```

A simple, fiber-rich hot chocolate with a warming kick from cayenne pepper. Cocoa powder is a natural source of dietary fiber — 2 tablespoons provides roughly 3–4g, comparable to a small apple — making this a genuinely nutritious daily ritual.

Yield: 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • Cinnamon, to taste
  • Cayenne pepper, to taste
  • Hot water, enough to fill mug 3/4 full
  • Half and half or milk, optional, for creaminess
  • Sweetener of choice, optional, to taste

Instructions

  1. Combine cocoa powder, cinnamon, and cayenne pepper in a large mug.
  2. Fill the mug 3/4 of the way with boiling hot water and stir well until cocoa powder is fully dissolved.
  3. Optionally add a splash of half and half or milk for extra creaminess, and sweetener to taste. Stir and enjoy.

Notes

Unsweetened cocoa powder is a natural, whole-food source of dietary fiber. Per USDA FoodData Central (FDC ID 169593), cocoa powder contains approximately 37g of fiber per 100g — meaning 2 tablespoons (about 10g) delivers roughly 3.7g of fiber. That is comparable to a medium apple (about 4g) and a meaningful contribution toward the recommended 25–38g daily intake.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (cocoa base only, not including optional milk or sweetener): 23 calories | Protein: 2g | Fat: 1.4g | Carbohydrates: 5.8g | Fiber: 3.7g

Source: USDA FoodData Central, FDC ID 169593. Based on 10g (2 tbsp) unsweetened cocoa powder.
```
