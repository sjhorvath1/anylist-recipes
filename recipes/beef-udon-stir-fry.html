Beef Udon Stir-Fry

Sirloin tenderized with baking soda, seared in a wok or skillet, then tossed with frozen udon noodles, onion, bell peppers, garlic, and a soy, oyster sauce, and Shaoxing wine stir-fry sauce.

Ingredients

Beef

  • 4 lb sirloin, thinly sliced against the grain
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 2 Tbsp neutral oil, plus more for cooking

Noodles + Vegetables

  • 5 packs frozen udon noodles
  • 1 large white onion, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
  • 12 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 bunch scallions, sliced

Stir-Fry Sauce

  • 1 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup oyster sauce
  • 6 Tbsp Shaoxing cooking wine
  • 2 Tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 4 tsp sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Toss the thinly sliced beef with the baking soda and let it sit for 15-20 minutes.
  2. Rinse the beef thoroughly with water to remove the baking soda, then pat it very dry. Toss with the salt, cornstarch, and 2 tablespoons oil.
  3. Whisk all of the sauce ingredients together and set aside.
  4. Heat a little neutral oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat. Sear the beef in batches so you do not overcrowd the pan, cooking until browned and slightly crispy around the edges. Remove and set aside.
  5. In the same pan, saute the onion, bell peppers, and garlic for 2-3 minutes.
  6. Add the frozen udon directly to the pan. You can let the noodles sit on the counter for about 10 minutes while you cook everything else, but there is no need to boil or completely defrost them first.
  7. Pour in the sauce and toss everything together as the noodles soften and separate, about 2-3 minutes.
  8. Return the beef to the pan, toss everything together, and finish with lots of scallions.
