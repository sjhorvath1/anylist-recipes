Creamy Italian Chicken Sausage Rigatoni

A lighter variation of the original Creamy Italian Sausage Rigatoni, made with Trader Joe's Sweet Italian Chicken Sausage in place of the seasoned lean ground beef, with a bag of frozen peas stirred in.

Makes 11 servings

Ingredients

Chicken Sausage

  • 1800g (4 lbs, about 20 links) Trader Joe's Sweet Italian Chicken Sausage, casings removed
  • 15g (1 Tbsp) olive oil

Pasta

  • 784g (28oz) rigatoni, or pasta of choice

Blended Cream Sauce

  • 900g (3 3/4 cups) cottage cheese
  • 336g (1, 12oz can) fat-free evaporated milk
  • 120g (1 cup) Parmigiano Reggiano, grated
  • 100g (1 cup) Fontina, shredded
  • 60g (1/2 cup) Pecorino Romano, grated

Aromatics

  • 3 yellow onions, diced
  • 20 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp red chili flakes
  • 1 tsp crushed fennel
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 5g (1 tsp) olive oil

Vegetables

  • 454g (1 lb) frozen peas

Garnish

  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Fresh chives, chopped

Instructions

  1. Blend 900g (3 3/4 cups) cottage cheese, 336g (1, 12oz can) fat-free evaporated milk, 120g (1 cup) Parmigiano Reggiano, 100g (1 cup) Fontina, and 60g (1/2 cup) Pecorino Romano until smooth. Set aside.
  2. Heat 15g (1 Tbsp) olive oil in a large pot. Remove the casings from the chicken sausage, add to the pot in an even layer, and cook until browned all the way through, breaking apart as it cooks. Remove the meat.
  3. Cook 784g (28oz) rigatoni in salted water.
  4. In the same pot, cook 3 diced yellow onions until browned. Add 20 cloves minced garlic, 2 tsp red chili flakes, 1 tsp crushed fennel, 1/2 tsp black pepper, and 5g (1 tsp) olive oil. Cook 2-3 minutes.
  5. Return the chicken sausage to the pot, reduce heat to low, and stir in the cream sauce. Add the frozen peas and pasta to the sauce and toss to coat, cooking briefly until the peas are heated through. Add chopped parsley and chopped chives. Thin with pasta water or milk if needed. Divide into 11 servings.
