Chicken Bacon Ranch Pockets

Source: @tastyshreds

```

High-protein stuffed dough pockets made with Greek yogurt-based dough, filled with grilled chicken, crispy bacon, two kinds of mozzarella, and a creamy ranch garlic sauce. Air-fried until golden and crispy. Makes 10 meal-prep pockets.

Yield: 10 pockets

Ingredients

Dough

  • 600g nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 280g self-rising flour
  • 2 cups Fit Flour protein flour

Filling

  • 21 oz (595g) raw chicken breast
  • 10 slices center cut bacon
  • 200g fat free mozzarella, shredded
  • 300g low moisture mozzarella, shredded

Ranch Garlic Sauce

  • 200g fat free cottage cheese
  • 100g 1/3 less fat cream cheese
  • Splash of milk
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2-3 tablespoons ranch seasoning

Instructions

  1. Mix together the Greek yogurt, self-rising flour, and Fit Flour protein flour until a dough forms.
  2. Split dough into 10 equal balls and roll each one out thin.
  3. Season chicken breast however you like, grill until cooked through, then cube into bite-sized pieces.
  4. Bake bacon at 425 degrees F for about 20 minutes until crispy, then chop.
  5. Blend together cottage cheese, cream cheese, milk, garlic cloves, and ranch seasoning until smooth and creamy.
  6. In a large bowl, combine cubed chicken, chopped bacon, both mozzarella cheeses, and the ranch garlic sauce.
  7. Add filling to each dough round, fold over, and seal edges tightly.
  8. Air fry at 400 degrees F for about 5 minutes or until golden brown and crispy.
  9. Let cool slightly before serving.
```
