Asian Mom's Lazy Salmon Rice

A simple one-pot salmon and rice dish. The salmon fillet cooks directly on top of the rice in a rice cooker, then is broken apart and mixed with furikake, sriracha, and Kewpie mayo. Served with seaweed sheets, cucumber, avocado, and kimchi for wrapping.

Rice and Salmon

  • 1 cup medium-grain white rice
  • 6 oz salmon fillet
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons furikake
  • 2 tablespoons sriracha
  • 2 tablespoons Kewpie mayo
  • Soy sauce, a splash, optional

Toppings

  • 1 packet seaweed sheets
  • 2 Persian cucumbers, sliced
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/4 cup kimchi
  1. Rinse and drain the rice 3 to 5 times, or until the water runs clear.
  2. Add the rice and the appropriate amount of water to your rice cooker.
  3. Place the salmon fillet directly on top of the rice.
  4. Cook using your preferred setting (the Quick Cook setting on a Zojirushi rice cooker works well).
  5. Once the rice is cooked, add green onions, furikake, sriracha, and Kewpie mayo.
  6. Gently break apart the salmon and mix everything together.
  7. Serve with more sriracha and Kewpie mayo, plus seaweed sheets, cucumbers, avocado, and kimchi.
