Shabushabu Gyudon Rice Cooker Bowl

A one pot rice cooker gyudon made with thinly sliced shabushabu beef, jasmine rice, and a soy dashi broth, topped with a jammy egg and green onion.

Yield: 2 servings

Ingredients

Rice Cooker Base

  • 10 oz thinly sliced eye of round beef, shabushabu style
  • 2/3 cup jasmine rice, uncooked
  • 1/3 cup beef bone broth
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1/2 tsp dashi powder
  • 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 1/2 tbsp fresh ginger, minced

Toppings

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 green onion, thinly sliced

Instructions

  1. Thinly slice the green onion.
  2. Rinse the jasmine rice under water.
  3. In a rice cooker, combine the uncooked rice, sliced beef, beef bone broth, soy sauce, mirin, dashi powder, yellow onion, and ginger.
  4. Cook for 30 minutes in the rice cooker.
  5. Meanwhile, boil the eggs in a pot of boiling water for 6 minutes, then immediately transfer to an ice bath to make jammy eggs.
  6. Once the rice cooker is done, divide the gyudon mixture between two bowls, top each with a jammy egg, and finish with the sliced green onion.

Nutrition

As reported in source, per serving: 528 calories | 47 g protein | 59 g carbohydrates | 10 g fat
