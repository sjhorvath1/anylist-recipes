Peanut Noodle Salad with Melted Peanut Dressing

A cold pasta salad tossed with crunchy vegetables, peanuts, and edamame, coated in a silky melted peanut butter dressing thinned with starchy pasta water.

Ingredients

The Salad Base

  • 8 oz pasta (rotini, spaghetti, or linguine)
  • 2 cups cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1/4 to 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 3 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 cucumber, shredded
  • 1 large carrot, shredded
  • 1/2 cup dry roasted edamame
  • 1/4 cup peanuts, chopped

The Melted Peanut Dressing

  • 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 3 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp fresh lime juice (about 1 lime)
  • 1 to 2 tbsp Fly By Jing mild chili crisp
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1/4 cup starchy pasta water, saved right before draining

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water. Right before draining, scoop out 1/4 cup of the starchy pasta water and reserve it. Drain the pasta well, but do not rinse.
  2. Microwave the peanut butter for 15 to 20 seconds to soften it. Whisk in the soy sauce, lime juice, mild chili crisp, and toasted sesame oil. Slowly whisk in the warm reserved pasta water until the dressing is silky, emulsified, and pourable.
  3. Add the warm pasta, shredded cabbage, cilantro, green onions, bell pepper, cucumber, and carrot to a large mixing bowl.
  4. Pour the peanut dressing over the top, add the chopped peanuts and dry roasted edamame, and toss everything together until perfectly coated.
