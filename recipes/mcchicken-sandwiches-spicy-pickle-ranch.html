McChicken Sandwiches with Spicy Pickle Ranch

Source: @diningwithskyler

Crispy baked ground chicken cutlets layered with a tangy, spicy dill pickle ranch, crunchy shredded lettuce, and soft potato buns. Bold, messy, and packed with briny flavor — the spicy, tangy cool-ranch cousin to your favorite fast-food sandwich, made at home and big enough to feed a whole crew in one slab.

Prep time: 15 minutes  |  Cook time: 25 minutes  |  Total time: 40 minutes  |  Yield: 6 sandwiches

Ingredients

Chicken Cutlet

  • 1 lb ground chicken or ground turkey
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • All-purpose flour, for dusting
  • 2 eggs, whisked
  • Panko breadcrumbs, enough to coat
  • Avocado oil spray

Spicy Pickle Ranch

  • 3/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup pickle juice, or to taste
  • 1-2 tbsp hot sauce, or to taste
  • 1/3 cup finely chopped pickles (bread and butter or dill)
  • Handful fresh dill, finely chopped (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Kosher salt, to taste
  • Lemon juice, to taste (optional)

Assembly

  • 6 Martin's potato buns
  • Shredded iceberg lettuce, to taste
  • Bread and butter or dill pickles, sliced, enough for layering
  • American cheese slices (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F.
  2. In a bowl, mix the ground chicken with salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika until just combined.
  3. On a sheet of parchment paper, press the chicken mixture into a thin rectangle large enough to match your bun layout — about a quarter inch larger than the buns so there is some overhang.
  4. Lightly dust the top of the chicken slab with flour, brush generously with the whisked egg, then coat fully in panko breadcrumbs, pressing gently to adhere.
  5. Spray generously with avocado oil until fully coated. Carefully flip the slab and repeat on the other side.
  6. Transfer to a sheet pan and bake for 15 minutes, then broil for a few minutes until golden and crispy. Keep a close eye on it — broiler times vary widely (as little as 1-2 minutes on a strong broiler, up to 5 minutes on a weaker one).
  7. While the chicken bakes, make the spicy pickle ranch: mix mayo with pickle juice, hot sauce, chopped pickles, fresh dill (if using), garlic powder, onion powder, black pepper, and salt. Add lemon juice if desired. Taste and adjust until creamy, tangy, and slightly loose.
  8. Arrange buns for slicing: place 4 buns attached side by side, then 2 next to them to form one large slab. Slice horizontally to separate tops from bottoms.
  9. Layer the sandwiches: spread sauce on the bottom buns, lay the crispy chicken cutlet on top, add more sauce, then pickle slices, shredded lettuce, and American cheese if using. Close with the top buns.
  10. Slice the slab into 6 individual sandwiches and serve immediately.
