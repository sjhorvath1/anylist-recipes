Frozen Bean & Cheese Burritos

```

High-protein freezer burritos packed with seasoned pinto beans and three cheeses. Make a batch of 15 and reheat from frozen whenever you need a quick, filling meal.

Yield: 15 burritos

Ingredients

Beans

  • 30g (2 Tbsp) butter
  • 10 cloves garlic, minced or grated
  • 2250g (5 cans) pinto beans, drained
  • 800g (2 jars) green enchilada sauce
  • 113g (4 oz / 1 can) green chiles
  • 1 Tbsp nutritional yeast
  • 1 Tbsp onion powder
  • 1 Tbsp salt, adjust to taste

Cheese

  • 360g (1 1/2 cups) cottage cheese
  • 300g (3 cups) fat-free mozzarella, shredded
  • 226g (2 cups / 1 block) Monterey Jack, shredded

Tortillas

  • 15 low-carb burrito-size tortillas

Instructions

  1. Heat a large pot over medium heat. Add butter and garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Add drained pinto beans, green enchilada sauce, green chiles, nutritional yeast, onion powder, and salt. Cook over medium-low heat for about 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until very thick and all liquid has reduced. Mash the beans directly in the pot until thick and cohesive.
  2. Add the cottage cheese, mozzarella, and Monterey Jack. Stir until fully melted and combined.
  3. Transfer the filling to a bowl and let cool for 15 to 20 minutes. The longer you let the filling cool and thicken, the easier the burritos will be to roll.
  4. Lay out the tortillas and divide the filling evenly between them (about 165g filling per burrito), then roll tightly.
  5. Optionally, toast in a pan until lightly golden to improve flavor. You can also skip this step and pan-fry when reheating instead. Freeze the burritos in a single layer, then transfer to bags or containers.

Reheat Instructions (from frozen)

Microwave for 2 minutes, then air fry at 350 degrees F for 6 to 7 minutes until the tortilla is crispy and the interior is warmed all the way through.

Nutrition

Per burrito (as provided by recipe author): 380 calories | 33g protein | 62g carbohydrates (33g fiber) | 14g fat

```
