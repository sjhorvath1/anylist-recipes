Spicy Peanut Sauce Noodles

Marinated chicken and noodles tossed in a spicy peanut sauce made with sun-dried tomatoes, sriracha, and lime juice.

Yield: 10 servings

Ingredients

Chicken/Marinade

  • 1120g (40oz) chicken breast, diced into bite-sized cubes
  • 70g (5 Tbsp) soy sauce
  • 50g (3 Tbsp) sriracha
  • 20g (4 tsp) lime juice
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp red chili flakes

Aromatics

  • 20 cloves garlic, minced
  • 10 green onions, whites and greens separated

Peanut Sauce

  • 200g (3/4 cup) peanut butter
  • 80g (5 Tbsp) soy sauce
  • 40g (3 Tbsp) sriracha
  • 150g (7 1/2 Tbsp) honey
  • 80g (1/2 cup) sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 90g (6 Tbsp) lime juice
  • 160g (2/3 cup) water

Pasta

  • 600g (22oz) noodles of choice

Instructions

  1. Dice 1120g (40oz) chicken breast into bite-sized cubes. In a bowl, combine with 70g (5 Tbsp) soy sauce, 50g (3 Tbsp) sriracha, 20g (4 tsp) lime juice, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp onion powder, and 1/2 tsp red chili flakes. Marinate for at least 20 to 30 minutes.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together 200g (3/4 cup) peanut butter, 80g (5 Tbsp) soy sauce, 40g (3 Tbsp) sriracha, 150g (7 1/2 Tbsp) honey, 80g (1/2 cup) chopped sun-dried tomatoes, and 90g (6 Tbsp) lime juice until smooth. Set aside.
  3. Boil 600g (22oz) noodles of choice according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  4. In a large nonstick pan over medium-high heat, cook the marinated chicken for 4 to 5 minutes. Add 20 cloves minced garlic and the whites of the 10 green onions, and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes until fragrant and the chicken is fully cooked. Remove and set aside.
  5. Reduce heat to low. In the same pan, add the peanut sauce and 160g (2/3 cup) water. Stir to thin slightly. Add the cooked noodles and toss everything in the sauce. Simmer for 2 to 3 minutes to absorb flavor. Return the cooked chicken to the pan and toss.
  6. Garnish with the green parts of the green onions before serving. Enjoy, and freeze leftovers.

Nutrition

Per serving (makes 10): 510 calories | 42g protein | 60g carbohydrates | 13g fat
