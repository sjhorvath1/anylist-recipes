Lazy Pork & Shrimp Potstickers

Skip the folding: a ginger-garlic pork and shrimp filling is scooped straight into the skillet, topped with a wrapper, then steamed and crisped. Makes about 24-30 potstickers (yield and serving count are estimated, not stated in the source).

Yield: 5 servings

Ingredients

Filling

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1 lb raw shrimp, finely minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 5 scallions, sliced
  • 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 Tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

To Cook

  • 24-30 square dumpling or wonton wrappers
  • 2-3 Tbsp neutral oil
  • 1/2 cup water, per large skillet batch
  • Scallions, sliced, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Mix all of the filling ingredients together until well combined.
  2. Oil a large skillet, then scoop about 2 Tbsp of filling per dumpling directly into the pan.
  3. Lay a wrapper over each mound and press it around the filling.
  4. Add 1/2 cup water, cover, and steam for 6-8 minutes until cooked through.
  5. Uncover and cook until the bottoms are crisp.
  6. Finish with sliced scallions.
