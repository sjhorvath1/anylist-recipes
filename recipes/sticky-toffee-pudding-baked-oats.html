Sticky Toffee Pudding Baked Oats

```

Baked oats sweetened with mashed banana and maple syrup, topped with a blended date caramel sauce made with tahini. A nourishing, high-protein breakfast meal prep recipe inspired by sticky toffee pudding.

Yield: 4 servings

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Ingredients

Baked Oats

  • 2 bananas, mashed
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 400ml milk
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 240g porridge oats
  • 60g vanilla protein powder

Date Caramel

  • 240g medjool dates, pitted
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 60g tahini
  • 240ml milk
  • Salt, a generous pinch

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180C (350F).
  2. In a large bowl, combine the mashed banana, maple syrup, and 400ml milk.
  3. Add the ground cinnamon, baking powder, porridge oats, and protein powder. Stir until well combined.
  4. Pour the mixture into an ovenproof dish and bake for 40 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, prepare the date caramel. Heat the 240ml milk in a pan over medium heat until hot, then pour it over the pitted dates in a bowl. Let soak for 10 minutes.
  6. Transfer the soaked dates and milk to a blender. Add the vanilla extract, tahini, and salt. Blend until completely smooth and runny. Add more milk if the consistency is too thick.
  7. Once the baked oats are out of the oven, use a skewer to poke holes all over the top.
  8. Allow to cool for 5 minutes, then pour the date caramel evenly over the top.
  9. Leave to sit for a few minutes to absorb, then slice into 4 to 6 squares. Serve and enjoy.
```
