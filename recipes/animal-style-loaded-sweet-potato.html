Animal Style Loaded Sweet Potato

A high protein, lower sugar loaded sweet potato topped with seasoned ground beef, melted cheddar, caramelized onion, and an animal style sauce made with pickles and pepperoncini. Part of a Stupid Simple Sundays recipe series. Yields 2 huge servings, or can be split into 4 smaller servings.

Yield: 2 servings (can be split into 4)

Ingredients

Main

  • 1 large sweet potato
  • 1 lb 94/6 ground beef
  • 2 slices thin cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 cup dill pickles, chopped
  • 2 tbsp pepperoncini, chopped
  • 1 yellow onion, caramelized

Sauce

  • 4 tbsp sugar-free ketchup
  • 1 tbsp light mayo
  • 4 tbsp nonfat Greek yogurt
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp monk fruit sweetener
  • 4 tbsp dill pickles, chopped
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce

Instructions

  1. Dice the onion and add it to a pan with a little oil or butter. Slowly caramelize over low heat, adding a splash of water every 5 minutes or so to help speed it up.
  2. Poke holes all over the sweet potato, wrap it in a wet paper towel, and microwave for 10 to 12 minutes depending on size. You want it super soft to the touch.
  3. Cook the ground beef to your liking, then mix in the cheddar cheese slices until melted.
  4. Mix together all of the sauce ingredients.
  5. Split open the sweet potato, pile the beef and cheese, caramelized onion, chopped dill pickles, chopped pepperoncini, and sauce on top, and enjoy.

Nutrition

As stated by source, per huge serving (recipe yields 2 huge servings; halve these values if split into 4 smaller servings): 575 calories | 52g protein | 55g carbs | 17g fat
