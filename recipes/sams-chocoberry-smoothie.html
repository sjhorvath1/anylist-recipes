Sam's Chocoberry Smoothie

A chocolate berry protein smoothie made with frozen mixed berries, collagen protein powder, PBfit peanut butter powder, and Fairlife chocolate milk.

Yield: 1 serving

Ingredients

  • 2 cups frozen mixed berries
  • 4 Tbsp Vital Proteins collagen protein powder (1 serving)
  • 2 Tbsp PBfit peanut butter powder (1 serving)
  • 1 bottle Fairlife chocolate milk

Instructions

  1. Add the frozen mixed berries, collagen protein powder, PBfit peanut butter powder, and Fairlife chocolate milk to a blender and blend until smooth.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (USDA FoodData Central): 538 calories | 51 g protein | 11 g fat | 65 g carbohydrates | 17 g fiber

Assumes a 14 fl oz Fairlife chocolate milk bottle and 4 Tbsp collagen powder at 20 g.
