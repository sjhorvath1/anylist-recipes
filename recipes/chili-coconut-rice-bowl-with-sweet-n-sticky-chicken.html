Chili Coconut Rice Bowl with Sweet and Sticky Chicken

Source: @oxxjayceexxo

A rich, fragrant chili-coconut broth ladled over jasmine rice, topped with glossy soy-honey glazed chicken thighs, bok choy, cilantro, and chili oil. Bold, warming, and weeknight-friendly.

Yield: 2 bowls

Ingredients

For the Broth

  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1/2 large yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 5 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp chili crisp
  • 1 can (13.5 fl oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 lime, juiced
  • Sliced Thai chili, to taste (optional, for heat)
  • 1 head bok choy
  • Fresh cilantro, to finish
  • Chili oil, to finish
  • Sesame seeds, to finish
  • Jasmine rice, cooked, to serve

For the Sweet and Sticky Chicken

  • 4 boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch (for coating chicken)
  • Neutral oil, for searing

For the Sticky Sauce

  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 tsp cornstarch (whisked into sauce)

Instructions

Make the Broth

  1. Heat the neutral oil in a pot over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 2 to 3 minutes. Then add the garlic and ginger and saute until fragrant, about another 2 to 3 minutes.
  3. Add the chili crisp and toast for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk and chicken broth.
  5. Add the fish sauce, soy sauce, and brown sugar.
  6. Simmer for 10 to 15 minutes to develop flavor. While the broth simmers, prepare the chicken.
  7. In the last 5 minutes, add the lime juice and bok choy. Adjust heat and salt as needed.

Make the Chicken

  1. Pat the chicken thighs dry. Season with salt and pepper, then coat with 1 tbsp cornstarch.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together all sticky sauce ingredients including the 2 tsp cornstarch.
  3. Sear the chicken in a hot pan with oil for about 5 to 6 minutes per side until caramelized. Remove the chicken from the pan.
  4. In the same pan, add the sauce and simmer until syrupy.
  5. Return the chicken to the pan and coat in the sauce, reducing until the chicken is glossy and sticky. Remove and slice.

Build the Bowl

  1. Add cooked jasmine rice to each bowl. Pour the chili-coconut broth over the rice, then top with sliced chicken and bok choy. Garnish with cilantro, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of chili oil.
