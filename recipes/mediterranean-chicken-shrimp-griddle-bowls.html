Mediterranean Chicken and Shrimp Griddle Bowls

Blackstone griddle bowls with marinated chicken, shrimp, and charred zucchini, bell pepper, and red onion, served with warm pita and yellow rice. Extra shrimp is included so a pescatarian guest can build a full bowl without the chicken.

Yield: 5 servings

Ingredients

Marinade

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Protein

  • 1 lb chicken breast or thighs, diced
  • 1.5 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined

Vegetables

  • 2 zucchini, sliced
  • 3 bell peppers, chopped
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, for the griddle

Toppings and Sides

  • 1/2 cup feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1/2 cup kalamata olives
  • 1 cup tzatziki sauce
  • 5 pita breads, warmed
  • 1.5 cups yellow rice, cooked

Instructions

  1. Whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and black pepper in a bowl to make the marinade.
  2. Split the marinade between the chicken and the shrimp in separate bowls, keeping them separate for easy serving later. Let marinate for at least 15 minutes.
  3. Cook the yellow rice according to package directions while prepping the griddle.
  4. Heat a Blackstone griddle to medium-high heat and add a little olive oil.
  5. Add the zucchini, bell peppers, and red onion to one side of the griddle. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes until tender and slightly charred.
  6. Place the marinated chicken on the hot griddle. Cook for 5 to 7 minutes until fully browned and cooked through.
  7. Add the marinated shrimp to a clean zone of the griddle. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until pink and opaque.
  8. Warm the pita on the griddle during the last minute or two of cooking.
  9. Set out the rice and pita alongside the veggies, chicken, and shrimp so everyone can build bowls or stuffed pitas. Top with feta, kalamata olives, and a heavy dollop of tzatziki.

Notes

For a pescatarian guest, keep their portion of chicken and shrimp on separate zones of the griddle, or cook the shrimp first, to avoid cross-contact, then build their bowl or pita with shrimp only, skipping the chicken. The extra half pound of shrimp above covers that portion. Leftover chicken and shrimp keep well for meal prep.
