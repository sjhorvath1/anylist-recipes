Raspberry Almond Chia Pudding

Source: @mac.magician

```

A protein- and fiber-packed chia pudding built on the 2-2-2-2 formula: Greek yogurt, milk, raspberries, and chia seeds. Make a batch on Sunday and enjoy throughout the week. Great for digestion.

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Greek yogurt
  • 2 cups milk of choice
  • 2 cups raspberries (frozen works great)
  • 2 cups chia seeds (use 1.5 cups for a thinner pudding)
  • 1/4 cup honey, or to taste
  • 1/4 tsp almond extract
  • A few scoops collagen powder
  • Drizzle of rosehip syrup (optional)

Instructions

  1. Add the Greek yogurt, milk, raspberries, honey, almond extract, and collagen to a large bowl.
  2. Use an immersion blender to completely liquify the mixture.
  3. Stir in the chia seeds and let the mixture sit at room temperature for 30 minutes.
  4. Scoop into storage containers of choice. Add a drizzle of rosehip syrup if desired.
  5. Refrigerate and enjoy throughout the week.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (4 servings): 664 calories | Protein: 30g | Fat: 30g | Carbohydrates: 78g | Fiber: 30g

Source: USDA FoodData Central. Collagen, almond extract, and rosehip syrup not included in calculation due to unspecified amounts.
```
