Mushroom and Cheddar Turkey Burgers with Garlic-Chive Aioli

Juicy ground turkey burgers packed with grated portobello mushrooms and sharp cheddar, cooked on a grill or skillet and topped with jammy caramelized onions and Steph's garlic-chive aioli.

Yield: 6 burgers

Ingredients

Burgers

  • 2 lbs ground turkey
  • 2 portobello mushrooms, finely grated
  • 16 oz Beecher's Flagship cheese or sharp cheddar, freshly shredded
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, minced

Caramelized Onions

  • 2 yellow onions, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp salted butter
  • Pinch of salt

Garlic-Chive Aioli

  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • About 7 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 to 3 tbsp fresh chives, minced, plus more for garnish

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter with the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onions and a pinch of salt and saute until jammy and browned, about 20 minutes.
  2. Make the aioli: stir together the mayonnaise, lemon juice, minced garlic, kosher salt, and chives. Garnish with more chives.
  3. Finely grate the portobello mushrooms using a box grater.
  4. In a large bowl, gently combine the turkey, mushrooms, shredded cheese, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and parsley. Mix just until combined; do not overmix.
  5. Form into 6 large patties, pressing a slight indentation into the center of each.
  6. Cook on a lightly oiled grill or in a skillet over medium-high heat for about 5 to 6 minutes per side, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  7. Let the burgers rest for 3 minutes, then serve topped with the caramelized onions and garlic-chive aioli.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (1 of 6 burgers with onions and aioli, bun not included): 888 calories | 50 g protein | 73 g fat | 9 g carbohydrates | 1 g fiber
