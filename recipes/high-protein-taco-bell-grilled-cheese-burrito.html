High Protein Taco Bell Grilled Cheese Burrito

Source: @_aussiefitness

```

A high-protein meal prep burrito inspired by Taco Bell's Grilled Cheese Burrito. Makes 10 burritos with seasoned beef, Spanish-style tomato rice, nacho cheese, and sriracha mayo. Easy and delicious meal prep.

Yield: 10 burritos

Ingredients

Rice

  • 14 oz washed uncooked white rice (approximately 42 oz cooked weight; can substitute Spanish-style store-bought minute rice)
  • 5.3 oz tomato passata/puree
  • 5.3 oz tomato paste
  • 25.4 fl oz beef broth
  • 4 garlic cloves, diced
  • 1.5 tsp chicken stock powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin

Beef

  • 3.3 lbs extra lean ground beef
  • 1/2 brown onion, diced
  • 1 packet low sodium taco seasoning
  • 2-4 tsp chipotle paste (such as La Costena Chipotle in Adobo Sauce; adjust to spice tolerance)
  • 3.5 oz tomato paste
  • 3.5 oz tomato passata/puree
  • Fresh cilantro, to taste
  • 1/2 lime, juiced

Per Burrito (Assembly — makes 10 burritos)

  • 10 warm tortillas (Mission Carb Balance Tortillas)
  • 4.2 oz cooked rice per burrito (equally distributed from batch)
  • 5.3 oz cooked beef per burrito (equally distributed from batch)
  • 1.2 oz nacho cheese sauce per burrito (such as Rico's Gourmet)
  • 1.6 oz low fat Greek yogurt or low fat sour cream per burrito
  • 1 oz light sriracha mayonnaise per burrito
  • Fresh cilantro, to taste
  • 0.9 oz light grated cheddar cheese per burrito (such as Bega 50% Less Fat Grated Cheese)

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice: Combine the uncooked white rice, tomato passata, tomato paste, beef broth, diced garlic, chicken stock powder, black pepper, onion powder, and cumin in a pot or rice cooker. Cook until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed.
  2. Cook the beef: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, saute the diced brown onion until softened. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it apart as it cooks.
  3. Season the beef: Add the taco seasoning, chipotle paste, tomato paste, and tomato passata to the cooked beef. Stir to combine and simmer until the sauce thickens. Finish with fresh cilantro and lime juice.
  4. Assemble the burritos: Warm each tortilla. Layer 4.2 oz of rice and 5.3 oz of seasoned beef down the center. Add 1.2 oz nacho cheese sauce, 1.6 oz Greek yogurt or sour cream, 1 oz sriracha mayo, fresh cilantro, and 0.9 oz grated cheddar cheese.
  5. Fold and grill: Fold the tortilla tightly into a burrito. Place seam-side down on a hot skillet or griddle and grill until golden and the cheese on the outside is melted and crispy.
  6. Repeat for all 10 burritos. Wrap individually for meal prep and refrigerate or freeze.

Nutrition

Per burrito (10 total): 663 calories | 60.4g carbohydrates | 23g fat | 55g protein

```
