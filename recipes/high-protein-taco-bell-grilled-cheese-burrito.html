High Protein Taco Bell Grilled Cheese Burrito

A high-protein meal prep burrito inspired by Taco Bell's Grilled Cheese Burrito. Makes 10 burritos with seasoned beef and chicken, Spanish-style tomato rice, Velveeta, and sriracha mayo — grilled until golden and crispy on the outside.

Yield: 10 burritos

Ingredients

Rice

  • 3 bags Knorr's Mexican Rice (or 14 oz dry white rice with ingredients below for approx. 42 oz cooked)
  • 5.3 oz tomato passata (puree)
  • 5.3 oz tomato paste
  • 25.4 fl oz beef broth
  • 4 garlic cloves, diced
  • 1.5 tsp chicken stock powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 0.5 tsp cumin

Meat Filling

  • 1 lb extra lean ground beef
  • 2 lb ground chicken
  • 0.5 brown onion, diced
  • 1 packet low-sodium taco seasoning
  • 6 tsp chipotle paste (such as La Costena Chipotle in Adobo Sauce; adjust to spice tolerance)
  • 3.5 oz tomato paste
  • 3.5 oz tomato sauce
  • Fresh cilantro, to taste
  • 0.5 lime, juiced

Burrito Assembly

  • 10 warm flour tortillas (Mission Carb Balance Tortillas recommended)
  • 1 jar pickled jalapenos
  • 10 slices Velveeta Cheese Singles
  • 3 tbsp low-fat Greek yogurt
  • 10 oz light sriracha mayonnaise
  • Fresh cilantro, to taste
  • 10 oz light grated cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice: Combine the uncooked white rice, tomato passata, tomato paste, beef broth, diced garlic, chicken stock powder, black pepper, onion powder, and cumin in a pot or rice cooker. Cook until the rice is tender and all liquid is absorbed.
  2. Cook the meat: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, saute the diced brown onion until softened. Add the ground beef and ground chicken and cook until browned, breaking it apart as it cooks.
  3. Season the meat: Add the taco seasoning, chipotle paste, tomato paste, and tomato sauce to the cooked meat. Stir to combine and simmer until the sauce thickens. Finish with fresh cilantro and lime juice.
  4. Assemble the burritos: Warm each tortilla. Layer 4.2 oz of rice and 5.3 oz of seasoned meat filling down the center. Add 1 slice of Velveeta, 1 oz sriracha mayo, a dollop of Greek yogurt, pickled jalapenos, fresh cilantro, and 1 oz grated cheddar cheese.
  5. Fold and grill: Fold the tortilla tightly into a burrito. Place seam-side down on a hot skillet or griddle and grill until golden and the cheese on the outside is melted and crispy.
  6. Repeat for all 10 burritos. Wrap individually for meal prep and refrigerate or freeze.
