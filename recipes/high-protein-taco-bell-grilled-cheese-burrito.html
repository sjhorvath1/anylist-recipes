High Protein Taco Bell Grilled Cheese Burrito

Source: @sjhorvath1

A high-protein meal prep burrito inspired by Taco Bell's Grilled Cheese Burrito. Makes 10 burritos with seasoned beef and chicken, Spanish-style tomato rice, nacho cheese, and sriracha mayo. Easy and delicious meal prep.

Yield: 10 burritos

Ingredients

Rice

  • 3 bags Knorr Mexican Rice, or 14 oz dry rice plus the ingredients below (yields approx 42 oz cooked)
  • 5.3 oz tomato passata or puree
  • 5.3 oz tomato paste
  • 25.4 fl oz beef broth
  • 4 garlic cloves, diced
  • 1.5 tsp chicken stock powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin

Meat Filling

  • 1 lb extra lean ground beef
  • 2 lb ground chicken
  • 1/2 brown onion, diced
  • 1 packet low sodium taco seasoning
  • 6 tsp chipotle paste, such as La Costena Chipotle in Adobo Sauce (adjust to spice tolerance)
  • 3.5 oz tomato paste
  • 3.5 oz tomato sauce
  • Fresh cilantro, to taste
  • 1/2 lime, juiced

Burrito Assembly

  • 10 warm Mission Carb Balance tortillas
  • 1 jar pickled jalapenos
  • 10 slices Velveeta Cheese Singles
  • 3 Tbsp low fat Greek yogurt
  • 20 Tbsp Hellmann's Light mayonnaise, mixed with sriracha to taste
  • Fresh cilantro, to taste
  • 10 oz reduced fat grated cheddar cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice: Combine the uncooked white rice, tomato passata, tomato paste, beef broth, diced garlic, chicken stock powder, black pepper, onion powder, and cumin in a pot or rice cooker. Cook until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed.
  2. Cook the meat: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, saute the diced brown onion until softened. Add the ground beef and ground chicken and cook until browned, breaking the meat apart as it cooks.
  3. Season the meat: Add the taco seasoning, chipotle paste, tomato paste, and tomato sauce to the cooked meat. Stir to combine and simmer until the sauce thickens. Finish with fresh cilantro and lime juice.
  4. Assemble the burritos: Warm each tortilla. Layer 4.2 oz of rice and 5.3 oz of seasoned meat down the center. Add 1.2 oz nacho cheese sauce, 1.6 oz Greek yogurt or sour cream, 2 Tbsp sriracha mayo, pickled jalapenos, fresh cilantro, and 1 oz grated reduced fat cheddar cheese.
  5. Fold and grill: Fold the tortilla tightly into a burrito. Place seam-side down on a hot skillet or griddle and grill until golden and the cheese on the outside is melted and crispy.
  6. Repeat for all 10 burritos. Wrap individually for meal prep and refrigerate or freeze.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (1 of 10 burritos): 677 calories | 48.8 g protein | 30.5 g fat | 67.0 g carbohydrates | 19.0 g fiber
