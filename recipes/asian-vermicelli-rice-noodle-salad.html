Asian Vermicelli Rice Noodle Salad

A fresh, vibrant noodle salad packed with crunchy vegetables and edamame, tossed in a bold sesame-soy dressing with chili crisp. Ready in 20 minutes with no cooking required beyond the noodles.

Prep time: 20 minutes

Servings: 6

Ingredients

For the salad

  • 12 oz vermicelli rice noodles
  • 1 cup cucumber, thinly sliced into half-moons
  • 1 cup carrots, grated
  • 1 cup red cabbage, shredded
  • 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 4 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 8 oz shelled edamame, cooked according to package instructions

For the dressing

  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 1 tbsp chili crisp

For garnish

  • Cilantro, to taste
  • Wonton strips, to taste
  • Sliced jalapenos, to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the vermicelli rice noodles according to package instructions. Drain and rinse with cold water, then set aside.
  2. In a large bowl, add the cooked noodles, cucumber, carrots, red cabbage, bell pepper, green onions, and shelled edamame.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, lime juice, garlic, rice vinegar, sesame oil, ginger, and chili crisp until combined.
  4. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss until everything is evenly coated.
  5. Garnish with cilantro, wonton strips, and sliced jalapenos. Serve immediately.
