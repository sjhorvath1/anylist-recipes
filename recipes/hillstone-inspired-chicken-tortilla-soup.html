Hillstone Inspired Chicken Tortilla Soup

Source: @heb

A silky, restaurant-style chicken tortilla soup thickened with masa and blended tortilla chips, strained until completely smooth, then finished with chicken and topped with avocado, cheddar, crispy tortilla strips, and cilantro.

Serves: 4 servings

Prep time: 10 minutes

Cook time: 30 minutes

Total time: 40 minutes

Ingredients

Soup

  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 1 medium yellow onion, small diced
  • Garlic, minced, to taste
  • 1/2 cup masa harina
  • 1 Tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 6 oz corn tortilla chips, blended or crushed
  • 8 cups chicken stock
  • 16 oz cooked chicken, shredded or chopped
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped

Toppings

  • 4 oz cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 200 g avocado, cut into pieces
  • 4 oz fried tortilla strips
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat the butter over medium heat in a large pot.
  2. Add the onion and garlic and cook 8 to 10 minutes, until very soft but not browned.
  3. Add the masa harina, cumin, chili powder, and black pepper. Cook about 1 minute, stirring constantly.
  4. Slowly whisk in the chicken stock.
  5. Add the crushed tortilla chips and simmer 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Blend until completely smooth.
  7. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer and return to the pot. Season to taste.
  8. Add the cooked chicken and heat gently for a few minutes.
  9. Stir in the cilantro.
  10. Serve topped with avocado, shredded cheddar, crispy tortilla strips, and cilantro.
