Hillstone-Inspired Chicken Tortilla Soup

A silky, restaurant-style chicken tortilla soup thickened with masa and blended tortilla chips, strained until completely smooth, then finished with chicken and classic toppings.

Serves: 4 servings

Prep time: 10 minutes

Cook time: 30 minutes

Total time: 40 minutes

Ingredients

Soup

  • Butter or fat of choice
  • 1 medium yellow onion, small diced
  • Garlic, minced, to taste
  • 1/2 cup masa harina
  • 1 Tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 Tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 6 oz corn tortilla chips, blended or crushed
  • 8 cups chicken stock
  • Cooked chicken, shredded or chopped
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped

Toppings

  • Avocado pieces
  • Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Fried tortilla strips

Instructions

  1. Heat the fat of your choice over medium heat in a large pot.
  2. Add the onion and garlic and cook 8 to 10 minutes, until very soft but not browned.
  3. Add the masa harina, cumin, chili powder, and black pepper. Cook about 1 minute, stirring constantly.
  4. Slowly whisk in the chicken stock. Add the crushed tortilla chips and simmer 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Blend until completely smooth, then strain through a fine-mesh strainer. Return to the pot and season to taste. Add the cooked chicken and heat gently for a few minutes. Stir in cilantro.
  6. Serve topped with avocado, shredded cheddar cheese, crispy tortilla strips, and cilantro.
