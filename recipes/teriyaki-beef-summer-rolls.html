Teriyaki Beef Summer Rolls

Fresh rice paper rolls filled with teriyaki ground beef, butter lettuce, mini cucumber, green onion, and cilantro, served with a peanut powder dipping sauce. High protein, low calorie option.

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

Beef Filling

  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/4 cup chopped green onion
  • 1/2 cup G Hughes sugar free teriyaki marinade

Rolls

  • 4 spring roll wrappers, rice paper
  • Butter lettuce leaves
  • 2 mini cucumbers, sliced
  • 1 bunch cilantro

Peanut Dipping Sauce

  • 1/2 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons PB2 powdered peanut butter
  • 1 tablespoon sriracha
  • 1 teaspoon chili crunch oil, optional

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together the minced garlic, soy sauce, PB2 powder, and sriracha for the dipping sauce, thinning with a splash of water to a pourable consistency. Stir in the chili crunch oil if using.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium high heat and cook the ground beef with the minced garlic for the filling, breaking it apart, until browned.
  3. Add the soy sauce, water, and G Hughes teriyaki marinade to the beef and simmer until the liquid reduces and coats the meat.
  4. Stir in the chopped green onion and remove the skillet from heat.
  5. Soften a rice paper wrapper in warm water, lay it flat, and layer with butter lettuce, sliced mini cucumber, cilantro, and the teriyaki beef mixture.
  6. Roll tightly, tucking in the sides, and repeat with the remaining wrappers and filling.
  7. Slice each roll in half and serve with the peanut dipping sauce.
