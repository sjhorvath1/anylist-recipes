Peach and Carrot Deli Container Mocktail

```

A refreshing, non-alcoholic deli-container drink made with a blended peach-carrot-ginger concentrate, fresh muddled peach and lime, mint, honey, and electrolyte water. Viral recipe by @olivianoceda. Makes one 32 oz drink.

Yield: 1 serving (32 oz)

Prep time: 15 minutes

Total time: 15 minutes

Ingredients

Concentrate

  • 1 peach, pitted and roughly chopped
  • 1/3 cup carrot juice
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 small knob fresh ginger
  • 2 cups water

For the Cup

  • 1 peach, pitted and diced
  • 1 lime, diced
  • Small handful fresh mint
  • 2 to 4 tablespoons honey
  • Ice
  • 1/3 cup electrolyte water (white peach flavor recommended)
  • Sparkling water to top (optional, in place of or in addition to electrolyte water)

Instructions

  1. Make the concentrate: combine 1 peach, the juice of 1 lime, 1 knob of ginger, 1/3 cup carrot juice, and 2 cups water in a blender. Blend until fully emulsified.
  2. Strain the concentrate through a fine mesh strainer into a separate container. Discard solids. The concentrate can be stored in the refrigerator for up to one week.
  3. Into a 32 oz deli container, add the diced peach, diced lime, fresh mint, and honey. Muddle everything together until the fruit releases its juices.
  4. Fill the container with ice.
  5. Pour in 1/3 cup of the concentrate, then fill the rest of the container with electrolyte water. You can also top with sparkling water instead of or in addition to electrolyte water.
  6. Stir or swirl to combine and enjoy immediately.
```
