Creamy Italian Sausage Rigatoni

Makes 11 servings

Ingredients

Low-Fat Italian Sausage

  • 1360g (48oz) 96% lean ground beef
  • 2 Tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 Tbsp black pepper
  • 1 Tbsp paprika
  • 1 Tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 Tbsp onion powder
  • 1 Tbsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp crushed fennel
  • 1 tsp red chili flakes
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 15g (1 Tbsp) olive oil

Pasta

  • 784g (28oz) rigatoni, or pasta of choice

Blended Cream Sauce

  • 900g (3 3/4 cups) cottage cheese
  • 336g (1, 12oz can) fat-free evaporated milk
  • 120g (1 cup) Parmigiano Reggiano, grated
  • 100g (1 cup) Fontina, shredded
  • 60g (1/2 cup) Pecorino Romano, grated

Aromatics

  • 3 yellow onions, diced
  • 20 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp red chili flakes
  • 1 tsp crushed fennel
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 5g (1 tsp) olive oil

Garnish

  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Fresh chives, chopped

Instructions

  1. Combine 2 Tbsp kosher salt, 1 Tbsp black pepper, 1 Tbsp paprika, 1 Tbsp garlic powder, 1 Tbsp onion powder, 1 Tbsp Italian seasoning, 1 Tbsp sugar, 2 tsp crushed fennel, 1 tsp red chili flakes, and 1 tsp baking soda. Add to 1360g (48oz) 96% lean ground beef, mix, and let rest at least 15 minutes.
  2. Blend 900g (3 3/4 cups) cottage cheese, 336g (1, 12oz can) fat-free evaporated milk, 120g (1 cup) Parmigiano Reggiano, 100g (1 cup) Fontina, and 60g (1/2 cup) Pecorino Romano until smooth. Set aside.
  3. Heat 15g (1 Tbsp) olive oil in a large pot. Add the seasoned beef in an even layer and cook until browned all the way through, then break apart and cook until fully browned. Remove the meat.
  4. Cook 784g (28oz) rigatoni in salted water.
  5. In the same pot, cook 3 diced yellow onions until browned. Add 20 cloves minced garlic, 2 tsp red chili flakes, 1 tsp crushed fennel, 1/2 tsp black pepper, and 5g (1 tsp) olive oil. Cook 2-3 minutes.
  6. Return the beef to the pot, reduce heat to low, and stir in the cream sauce. Add the pasta to the sauce and toss to coat, then add chopped parsley and chopped chives. Thin with pasta water or milk if needed. Divide into 11 servings.

Nutrition

Per serving: 625 calories | 55g protein | 61g carbohydrates | 17g fat
