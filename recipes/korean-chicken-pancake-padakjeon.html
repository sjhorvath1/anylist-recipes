Korean Chicken Pancake (Padakjeon)

A crisp pan-fried chicken and shrimp pancake topped with ice-crisped green onion strips and served with a soy-sesame dipping sauce. Recipe by Natalie DeWitt (Recipe No. 005).

Yield: Serves 2 to 3

Ingredients

Chicken Pancake

  • 1 package chicken breasts or thighs (about 1 1/2 lb / 680 g), cut into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 1/4 lb raw shrimp, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Whites from 1 bunch green onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 to 3 Fresno or serrano peppers, thinly sliced, seeds removed (optional)
  • 1/3 cup potato starch (cornstarch works too)
  • 1 tbsp cold water
  • Neutral oil, for frying

Dipping Sauce

  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Sesame seeds
  • Gochugaru
  • Green onion tops, sliced lengthwise into thin strips
  • Thinly sliced chili (optional)

Instructions

  1. Slice the green onions, separating the whites from the greens.
  2. Slice the green tops lengthwise into thin strips and place them in a bowl of ice water in the refrigerator. This keeps them incredibly crisp and mellows out some of their bite.
  3. Thinly slice the onion and peppers.
  4. In a large bowl, combine the chicken, shrimp, soy sauce, mirin, grated garlic, salt, pepper, green onion whites, onion, peppers, potato starch, and cold water.
  5. Mix everything together with a gloved hand until every piece of chicken is evenly coated.
  6. Whisk together the dipping sauce ingredients.
  7. Refrigerate everything until you get home.
  8. Heat a layer of neutral oil in a large stainless steel skillet over medium heat.
  9. Spread the chicken mixture into one thin, even pancake and let it cook undisturbed until the bottom is deeply golden brown and crispy, about 4 to 5 minutes.
  10. Carefully flip, drizzle a little more oil around the edges, and cook for another 4 to 5 minutes until the second side is crisp and the chicken is cooked through.
  11. Drain the green onions well and pile them over the hot pancake.
  12. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, gochugaru, and extra sliced chili if you want a little more heat.
  13. Serve immediately with the dipping sauce.
