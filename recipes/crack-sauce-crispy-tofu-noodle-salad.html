Crack Sauce and Crispy Tofu Noodle Salad

A rice noodle salad with sticky oven baked gochujang tofu, crunchy vegetables, and a tahini chili crack sauce dressing, topped with roasted peanuts.

Prep time: 15 minutes

Cook time: 30 minutes

Total time: 45 minutes

Yield: 4 servings

Ingredients

Crispy Gochujang Tofu

  • 400g extra-firm tofu, pressed and torn into chunks
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey (or maple syrup for vegan)
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar

Crack Sauce Dressing

  • 4 tbsp tahini
  • 2 tbsp crispy chili oil
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 lime, juiced

Noodle Salad

  • 200g rice noodles
  • 150g shelled edamame beans
  • 1 red pepper, finely sliced
  • 0.5 cucumber, sliced into thin half moons
  • 3 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 15g fresh coriander, roughly chopped

To Serve

  • 30g roasted peanuts, roughly chopped
  • Curled spring onions, optional
  • Extra coriander leaves
  • Extra crispy chili oil, optional

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 210°C fan.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the gochujang, soy sauce, honey, sesame oil and rice vinegar. Add the tofu and toss until well coated. Spread out on a lined baking tray and bake for 25 to 30 minutes, turning once halfway through, until sticky, caramelised and lightly crisp around the edges.
  3. Meanwhile, whisk together the tahini, crispy chili oil, soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup, garlic and lime juice until smooth. If needed, loosen with a splash of water until pourable.
  4. Cook the rice noodles according to the packet instructions. Drain, rinse under cold water and set aside.
  5. Add the noodles, edamame, red pepper, cucumber, spring onions and coriander to a large mixing bowl. Pour over most of the dressing and toss thoroughly until everything is evenly coated.
  6. Using tongs, twist the noodles into serving bowls to create a little height. Top with the crispy tofu, allowing a few pieces to tumble down the sides.
  7. Drizzle over the remaining dressing, then finish with the roasted peanuts, extra coriander, curled spring onions and a small drizzle of crispy chili oil if desired. Serve immediately.
