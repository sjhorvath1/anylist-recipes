Chipotle Chicken and Crispy Potatoes

Source: @jalalsamfit

Smoky chipotle-marinated chicken thighs served over crispy seasoned potatoes, all tossed in a creamy chipotle sauce. Perfect for weekly meal prep — makes 6 hearty servings. Marinate overnight for maximum flavor.

Yield: 6 servings

Cook Time: 25 minutes

Ingredients

Chipotle Chicken Marinade

  • 1200g raw boneless chicken thighs, excess fat trimmed
  • 2.5 tsp salt
  • 3 tsp dried parsley
  • 3 tsp garlic powder
  • 3 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2.5 tsp cumin
  • 3 tsp onion powder
  • 100g chipotle paste
  • 1.5 limes, juiced
  • 30g (6 tsp) olive oil

Crispy Seasoned Potatoes

  • 1300g raw yellow potatoes, cubed
  • 2.5 tsp garlic powder
  • 3 tsp Italian herbs
  • 3 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2.5 tsp onion powder
  • Avocado oil cooking spray

Creamy Chipotle Sauce

  • 20g light butter
  • 150-200g red onion, finely chopped
  • 1.5 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp Italian herbs
  • 60-80g chipotle paste (adjust to taste)
  • 400g light evaporated milk
  • 110g light cream cheese
  • Freshly chopped parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Trim excess fat from chicken thighs. Add all marinade seasonings, chipotle paste, lime juice, and olive oil. Mix well to fully coat. Marinate overnight if possible for best flavor.
  2. Heat a large pan on low and add butter and finely chopped red onion. Cook for 5-7 minutes until softened and lightly browned. Add the sauce seasonings and chipotle paste, mix well, and cook for another 5 minutes until caramelized.
  3. Keep the heat low and add evaporated milk and cream cheese to the pan. Stir continuously until the sauce is smooth, rich, and creamy.
  4. Cube the potatoes and toss with garlic powder, Italian herbs, smoked paprika, onion powder, and a heavy pass of avocado oil cooking spray. Mix well. Spread on a lined sheet pan and roast at 200C / 390F for 25 minutes until golden and crispy. Season with salt, then fold into the creamy chipotle sauce with fresh parsley until fully coated.
  5. Spread the marinated chicken on a sheet pan and oven bake at 200C / 390F for 25 minutes until golden and cooked through. Cut into cubes and serve over the creamy chipotle potatoes. Enjoy!

Nutrition

Per serving (6 total): 548 calories | 51g protein | 52g carbs | 17g fat
