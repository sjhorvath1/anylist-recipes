Caramelised Banana Bowl

A protein-packed bowl of Greek yogurt topped with pan-caramelised banana, honey, and cinnamon, finished with walnuts and dark chocolate.

Yield: 1 serving

Ingredients

Bowl

  • 340g Greek yogurt
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • 21g honey
  • Cinnamon powder, to taste
  • 10g walnuts
  • 20g dark chocolate

Instructions

  1. In a pan over medium heat, combine the sliced banana, honey, and a dusting of cinnamon powder. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the banana softens and caramelises.
  2. Spoon the Greek yogurt into a bowl.
  3. Top the yogurt with the caramelised banana mixture.
  4. Sprinkle with walnuts and dark chocolate to finish.

Nutrition
