High-Protein Hot Pockets

Source: @fairfiteats

Meal-prep-friendly high-protein hot pockets made with a Greek yogurt dough and filled with lean ground beef, scrambled eggs, bacon, and fat-free cheese. Freezer-ready for up to 3 months.

Yield: 5 hot pockets

Ingredients

```

Dough

  • 500g self-rising flour
  • 520g 0% fat Greek yogurt
  • 15g garlic salt (about 1 Tbsp)
  • 8g Italian seasoning (about 1 Tbsp)

Filling

  • 565g lean ground beef, 96/4
  • 100g diced white onion (about 0.5 cup)
  • 28g taco seasoning packet (1 oz)
  • 10g minced garlic (about 1 Tbsp)
  • 120g red enchilada sauce (about 0.5 cup)
  • 112g fat-free cheddar cheese, for beef filling (about 1 cup)
  • 500g whole eggs (about 10 large eggs)
  • 15g garlic salt (about 1 Tbsp)
  • 7g black pepper (about 1 Tbsp)
  • 112g fat-free cheddar cheese, for eggs (about 1 cup)
  • 50g cooked center-cut bacon slices (about 6 slices)
  • 10g fresh chives, chopped
  • 280g fat-free mozzarella (about 2.5 cups)
```

Instructions

  1. Make the dough: combine the self-rising flour, Greek yogurt, garlic salt, and Italian seasoning in a large bowl. Mix until a dough forms. Divide into 5 equal portions.
    2. ```
  2. Cook the beef filling: heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the ground beef with the diced white onion and minced garlic. Add the taco seasoning and red enchilada sauce. Stir to combine and cook until the sauce is absorbed. Remove from heat and stir in the fat-free cheddar cheese for the beef. Let cool.
  3. Scramble the eggs: whisk the eggs with garlic salt and black pepper. Cook in a nonstick skillet over medium heat until just set. Stir in the fat-free cheddar cheese for the eggs, the cooked bacon slices, and fresh chives. Let cool.
  4. Assemble the hot pockets: on a floured surface, roll each dough portion into a rectangle. Spoon a portion of the beef filling and egg filling onto one half of the dough. Add fat-free mozzarella on top. Fold the dough over the filling and press the edges firmly to seal. Crimp with a fork.
  5. Bake at 375 degrees F (190 degrees C) for 20-25 minutes, or until the dough is golden and cooked through.
  6. To freeze: wrap each hot pocket in aluminum foil or place in an airtight freezer bag. Freeze for up to 3 months.
  7. To reheat from frozen: wrap the hot pocket in a damp paper towel and microwave on a plate for 3-4 minutes. For a crispy exterior, finish in a pan or air fryer for a few minutes.
```
