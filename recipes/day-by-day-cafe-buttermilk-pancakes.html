Day by Day Cafe Buttermilk Pancakes

Source: Day by Day Cafe

Day by Day Cafe's buttermilk pancake batter, scaled down to 1/10 of the restaurant prep batch for home cooking.

Yield: 4 servings (about 12 pancakes, 3 per serving)

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 Tbsp oil
  • 1/3 cup whole milk

Instructions

  1. Sift together the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, egg, oil, and whole milk.
  3. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix completely. Prep only as much as you will use the same day.

Nutrition

Estimated per serving (3 pancakes): 265 calories | 8.3 g protein | 9.7 g fat | 36.3 g carbohydrates | 1.1 g fiber | 932 mg sodium
