Honey Miso Chicken Thighs

Air fryer chicken thighs marinated in a savory-sweet honey miso glaze, served over white rice with a sesame cucumber salad. Meal preps into 3 equal portions.

Yield: 3 servings

Cook Time: 14–16 minutes

Ingredients

Chicken Marinade

  • 600g raw skinless chicken thighs (200g per serving)
  • 1 Tbsp minced garlic
  • 3 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 5ml sesame oil
  • 45g white miso paste
  • 15g honey
  • 1 tsp sweetener (such as stevia or erythritol)
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Salt, to taste

Cucumber Salad

  • 1 large cucumber, sliced
  • 1 tsp garlic powder or minced garlic
  • 1 tsp brown stevia (or sweetener of choice)
  • 5ml sesame oil
  • 5ml soy sauce
  • Pinch of gochugaru or chili powder
  • Green onions, to taste
  • Sesame seeds, to taste
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

To Serve

  • 375g cooked white rice (125g per serving)
  • Green onion, sliced, for garnish
  • Sesame seeds, for garnish

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the 600g chicken thighs, garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, white miso, honey, sweetener, black pepper, and salt. Mix until the chicken is fully coated.
  2. Let the chicken marinate for a minimum of 30 minutes and up to 24 hours in the refrigerator.
  3. Cook the marinated chicken thighs in an air fryer at 370°F for 14–16 minutes, until cooked through.
  4. While the chicken cooks, prepare the cucumber salad: in a container combine the sliced cucumber, garlic powder, brown stevia, sesame oil, soy sauce, gochugaru or chili powder, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  5. Divide everything into 3 equal portions and serve each over 125g of cooked white rice. Garnish with sesame seeds and sliced green onions.
